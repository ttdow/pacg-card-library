Experimental PACG adventure map

With thanks to PathfinderWiki Mapping Project contributors, John Mechalas,
Oznogon, and PathfinderWiki contributors.

Selected factual place coordinates: https://github.com/pf-wikis/mapping
Full atlas terrain: https://github.com/pf-wikis/mapping-data
The adapted geographic datasets are distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
Modifications: geometry simplification, coordinate rounding, longitude seam
splitting, Mercator pole clipping, coastline extraction, factual place labels,
original flat styles, and PACG scenario overlays. See sources.json for input
checksums and the pinned geographic source commit.
