This map is made possible by the excellent work of the PathfinderWiki Mapping Project. Thank you to its contributors and the PathfinderWiki community for building and sharing this wonderful resource!

Special thanks to John Mechalas for the foundational GIS compilation and Oznogon for contributions to the earlier GIS project and its original interactive map. Their work, and the mapping team's continuing efforts, make exploring Golarion possible.

Place coordinates come from the mapping repository and terrain geometry from its Kart data repository. Both follow CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. With gratitude to their contributors, our adapted geographic datasets are distributed under the same license.

Our changes include geometry simplification, coordinate rounding, splitting shapes at the longitude seam, clipping at the Mercator polar limits, coastline extraction, factual place labels, original flat styling, and PACG scenario markers. All geographic layers are included. Borders show the latest open-ended source records; historical variants remain in the dataset. This experiment remains separate from the main library navigation.

PACG scenario names and card transcriptions: Pathfinder Adventure Card Game Wiki. Source links and retained revisions accompany every scenario. Scenario popups use the library's rules-only card text and Original/Errata versions; flavour text, article prose, and raster map artwork are excluded.

The catalog covers the four original boxed campaigns: 28 adventures and 140 scenarios, including the two printed bonuses. Regional markers show broad context, and entrance markers identify access to extradimensional settings. Off-world and unverified settings remain selectable without invented Golarion coordinates. All 935 source cities and 1,803 source points of interest are retained. Their point markers are available at every zoom, without clustering or sampling; text labels adapt to zoom and overlap.

Dotted paths show scenario progression rather than roads or exact travel routes. Arrowheads indicate required order. Groups without arrows may be completed in any order; all group members must be completed before their directed finale. Progression follows the adventure scenario lists and retained order instructions, recorded with source revisions in the adventure catalog. Off-map scenarios remain in the list without invented map connections.

Thank you to Paizo and the wonderful artists whose illustrations bring these adventures to life! Every scenario has an accompanying illustration selected from publicly posted Paizo Blog artwork. Artists, publisher sources, copyright notices, and exact crops are recorded in our scenario artwork register. Shared or contextual illustrations are identified in their captions; they do not always depict the exact scenario.
