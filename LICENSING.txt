MapLibre retains the notices in map/vendor/LICENSE.txt. The accompanying

map/artwork.json. No original raster map artwork is included. These materials

metadata or accompanying notices; no individual credits have been inferred.

source links, and name mappings. The original PNG bytes are preserved.
