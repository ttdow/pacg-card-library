The community behind the reference

Our thanks to the volunteer editors and contributors of the Pathfinder Adventure Card Game Wiki. Their inventories, transcriptions, and corrections made this reference possible.

Each card’s source record links to its wiki article; the article’s history credits its contributors. Retained revision metadata identifies the transcription used. Paizo’s published inventories and official errata provide additional verification where available.

Wiki-authored contributions are used under Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 3.0 Unported where applicable. Covered adaptations remain under that license. Underlying game text retains its own rights.

Our changes include extracting structured fields, normalizing text, separating rules from flavor text, and recording reviewed original and errata variants. The master rulebook reorganizes the four rulebooks into shared topics with internal variations.
